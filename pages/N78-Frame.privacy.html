Privacy Policy

N78-Frame

Free Windows utility · Local-first · No Nivo78 account · No cloud sync · No analytics

How N78-Frame handles information on your device.

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nivo78 Mobile Apps, LLC (“Nivo78,” “we,” “us”) handles information in N78-Frame (“the app”). N78-Frame is a free Windows utility for cropping and scaling images on your PC. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the app.

1. Summary

Your images stay on your device. N78-Frame does not upload pictures to Nivo78 or nivo78.com. It does not require a Nivo78 account, does not sync a photo library, does not phone home, and does not include advertising or behavioral analytics.

The only time Nivo78 receives information from you is when you choose to send support, feedback, or contact email. Those messages contain only what you write (plus any app version or OS line you leave in the draft).

2. Information processed locally

When you use N78-Frame, processing happens on this PC:

  • Images you open and save. Source and output files live in folders you choose. The app reads and writes image bytes only to perform framing work you start.
  • Session settings. Crop/Scale mode, size, zoom, pan, pad, and related controls stay in memory while the app runs. They are not uploaded.
  • Legal acceptance record. When you accept the Terms of Use, the app stores a small record (terms version, timestamp, app version) under your Documents folder (Documents\N78-Frame\) so you are not prompted on every launch.

3. Information you send outside the app

N78-Frame does not automatically send your images to Nivo78. You may choose to:

  • Save framed files to disk or removable media you control
  • Email support, feedback, or contact addresses from Help → Feedback or this website

4. What we do not collect

  • No Nivo78 account or login for core framing work
  • No cloud photo sync or background upload of images
  • No advertising identifiers or behavioral analytics
  • No silent diagnostics collection

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Do not use it to submit personal information about children to us by email.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: support@nivo78.com · contact@nivo78.com
