1. Nature of Software: Digital Calculation Aid & Reference Tool

N78-Machining is a digital engineering calculation utility, tooling reference database, GD&T solver, and shop organizational aid (analogous to a physical machinist handbook, dial caliper, or precision shop calculator).

All calculations, algorithms, cutting speed (\(V_c\)) baselines, feed rates (\(f_z\)), spindle speeds (RPM), radial chip thinning factors (RCTF), spindle horsepower/kilowatt estimates, cutting torque calculations, cantilever tool deflection models (\(\delta\)), tap drill recommendations, thread pitch calculations, and compiled G-code canned cycle snippets are mathematical approximations and theoretical reference starting points based on generalized material standards.

They do not constitute certified machining programs, nor do they replace physical workpiece inspection, material batch hardness testing, tooling manufacturer catalog limits, or the skilled professional judgment of a qualified machinist.

2. 100% Sole Operator & Shop Responsibility

The operator, machinist, CNC programmer, setup technician, and shop owner assume 100% sole responsibility for:

  • Feeds & Speeds Verification: Verifying all spindle speeds (RPM), surface cutting speeds (SFM / m/min), table feed rates (IPM / mm/min), and chip loads against actual machine spindle power/torque curves, tool manufacturer limits, and workpiece metallurgy.
  • Tool Assembly & Overhang: Inspecting tool stickout, gauge length, collet torque, shrink-fit holding force, tool pull-stud retention, and holder-to-part clearances before initiating cutting.
  • Workholding & Fixture Rigidity: Confirming vise clamping torque (e.g., 45–60 ft-lbs), clamp placement, stock seating against parallels, vacuum fixture seals, and chuck grip force.
  • Coordinate Offsets (WCS): Setting and physically validating Part Zero datums (G54 through G59, G54.1 P1–P48) and tool length offsets with edge finders, dial indicators, or calibrated touch probes.
  • Clearance Planes & G-Code Review: Reviewing all generated G-code, canned drilling/tapping cycles (G81, G83, G73, G84), cutter compensation lead-ins (G41/G42), and safe retract clearance planes (Z Retract) prior to execution.
  • Situational Awareness & Air Cuts: Maintaining active situational awareness with one hand on FEED HOLD and Rapid Override reduced to 5% or 25% on all first-article setup runs.

5. Mandatory 6-Point Pre-Flight Verification Checklist

Before pressing CYCLE START on any CNC machining center, lathe, or manual mill using calculations or G-code generated by this software, you agree to execute the following mandatory 6-point verification protocol:

  1. Spindle RPM & Feed Rate: Verified against physical tool catalog limits, workpiece alloy hardness, and spindle torque curves.
  2. Workpiece Clamping & Seating: Stock firmly clamped, parallels checked for tightness, and fixture torque inspected.
  3. Tool Stickout & Clearances: Minimum necessary tool stickout utilized; holder body and spindle nose visually cleared from clamps and vise jaws.
  4. Part Zero & Tool Offsets: WCS (G54–G59) datums and tool length offsets physically verified using touch probe, indicator, or edge finder.
  5. Safe Clearance Plane (Z Retract): Z retract plane verified well above all toe clamps, strap bolts, and fixture obstacles.
  6. Dry Run / Air Cut: First article run executed in single block mode or air-cut with Rapid Override set to 5% or 25%.
