2a. Licensed work and code compliance (electrical-specific)

N78-Electrical provides field and shop calculation aids (for example conduit fill, ampacity, voltage drop, box fill, and motor branch circuits). Results depend on the inputs you provide and the edition of codes and standards your jurisdiction adopts.

Only a qualified licensed electrician or engineer (as required by your jurisdiction) may design, install, alter, or energize electrical systems. You — not Nivo78 — are solely responsible for NEC/NFPA or local code compliance, permit requirements, inspections, arc-flash and shock safety, lockout/tagout, and every installation decision before energizing equipment.

The app does not replace manufacturer instructions, utility requirements, AHJ rulings, or professional engineering judgment on complex systems.
