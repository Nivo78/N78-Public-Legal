2a. The Professional Machinist Standard (machining-specific)

Just as a craftsman looking up speeds and feeds in Machinery’s Handbook or measuring with a digital micrometer remains 100% solely responsible for checking tool clearances, part zero, and workholding rigidity before cutting, the operator of N78-Machining retains sole responsibility for all machine setups and cut outcomes. A reference calculator provides starting baselines; the operator commands the spindle.

N78-Machining is a digital calculation utility, engineering reference, and shop organizational aid. Speeds, feeds, horsepower estimates, tap drill recommendations, canned cycles, and G-code snippets are mathematical approximations and reference starting points only — not certified machining programs.

The operator, programmer, and machinist bear 100% sole responsibility for verifying cutting parameters, spindle RPM, feed rates, chip loads, stepovers, depths of cut, tool stickout, clearances, workholding, coolant delivery, and G-code programs before cycling any CNC machine, manual mill, lathe, or cutting any material.

2b. Shop Calculation Record and export scope

An exported Shop Calculation Record is a tamper-evident digest of in-app license metadata and referenced calculation capabilities only. It is not AS9100, ISO 9001, ITAR, EAR, or NIST certification; not third-party audit evidence; and not a substitute for your quality system, export-compliance program, or machine setup verification. Nivo78 does not certify your shop, processes, or parts.

N78-Machining is designed for local-first, air-gapped use with no product telemetry on vault or job data. Your organization — not Nivo78 — is solely responsible for ITAR, EAR, export controls, defense/security policies, and determining whether this software is appropriate for a given facility or contract.
