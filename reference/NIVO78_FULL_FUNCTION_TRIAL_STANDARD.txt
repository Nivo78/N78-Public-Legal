NIVO78 MANDATORY STANDARD — FULL-FUNCTION TRIALS
================================================
Rule IDs: FAMILY-FULL-FUNCTION-TRIAL [STATIC], APP-TRIAL-FULL-FUNCTION [GATE]
Primary projections: 01-product-philosophy.mdc, 11-testing-quality.mdc, 17-web-marketing.mdc
Related: NIVO78_RELEASE_TAXONOMY.txt (R3/R4), NIVO78_STORE_TRIAL_READ_ONLY_EXPORT_LOCK.txt (post-trial),
NIVO78_PRICING_CLAIM_REGISTRY.json (claim: full_function_trial)

Applies to: Every Nivo78 product that offers a **time-limited trial**, regardless of trial duration
or whether the trial precedes a one-time purchase (R4) or subscription (R3 configuration).

Does not apply when the product has no time-limited trial (permanent free tier only, R1/R2 free paths).

================================================================================
REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

1. All advertised paid features MUST be available during the trial period.
2. Artificial feature restrictions and arbitrary usage caps MUST NOT be imposed solely to pressure conversion.
3. Customers MUST be able to evaluate the product using real-world workflows, subject to legitimate
   technical, legal, safety, and third-party service constraints.
4. Trial limitations MUST be disclosed before activation and MUST NOT be misrepresented as full functionality.
5. Trial expiration MUST NOT silently authorize a charge or subscription.
6. Customer-created data MUST remain reasonably accessible and exportable after expiration.
7. Trial duration, activation behavior, expiration behavior, purchase price, and applicable license
   terms MUST be clearly disclosed.
8. Automated tests MUST verify paid-feature access, usage-limit behavior, expiration, data retention,
   export, and applicable licensing transitions (where a trial exists).
9. The release gate MUST reject a trial configuration that introduces undocumented feature restrictions
   or artificial usage caps.
10. Any exception MUST have a documented justification, an identified approving authority, and clear
    customer-facing disclosure.

Advertising rule: A trial MUST NOT be advertised as full-function if a material portion of the
advertised paid functionality is unavailable during the trial.

Approved trial taglines and truthful-use rules: reference/NIVO78_TRIAL_MESSAGING.txt and
NIVO78_TRIAL_MESSAGING.json (FAMILY-TRIAL-TAGLINE-CANONICAL, APP-TRIAL-TAGLINE-TRUTH).
Taglines are permitted only when entitlements and disclosures match the claim.

================================================================================
VERIFICATION (APP-TRIAL-FULL-FUNCTION)
================================================================================

Family structure: reference file present; rule IDs in APP_STANDARD and registry.
Product GATE (when trial surface exists): guardrail or regression tests named or tagged for trial
transitions; docs/MARKETING_COPY or hub copy must not claim full-function while omitting disclosed limits.
Release gate product adapters exit non-zero when trial caps are undocumented in MARKETING_COPY.txt or
reference/NIVO78_FULL_FUNCTION_TRIAL_STANDARD.txt exception block is absent.

NOT APPLICABLE: Product documents no time-limited trial in product.identity.json (trial_offered: false)
and no trial entitlement implementation in shipping source.
