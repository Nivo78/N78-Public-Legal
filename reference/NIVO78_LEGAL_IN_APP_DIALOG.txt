NIVO78 LEGAL — IN-APP DIALOG CONTAINMENT
========================================
Rule ID: APP-LEGAL-IN-APP-DIALOG
Class: GATE (family store and business utilities with Privacy/Terms)
Primary projection: .cursor/rules/07-overrides-legal.mdc
Companion: APP-LEGAL-GATE, APP-UI-DIALOG-CANCEL-BUTTON, APP-UI-UNIVERSAL-TEXT-SELECTION

Principle
---------
Privacy Policy and Terms of Service MUST open inside the application. The operator
must not be sent to an external browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) to read legal text
during first-run gate, Help menu Privacy/Terms, or any in-product legal link.

Prohibited for Privacy and Terms
--------------------------------
- Desktop.getDesktop().browse() for policy/terms handoffs
- Intent.ACTION_VIEW on legal HTML assets or nivo78.com privacy/terms URLs from in-app chrome
- UIApplication.open() / LocalUriHandler.openUri() for Privacy/Terms from the app shell
- Extracting bundled HTML to a temp folder and opening it in the system browser

Required
--------
- Dedicated in-app modal (Compose Dialog / DialogWindow) with:
  - Scrollable, selectable document body (SelectionContainer on read-only text)
  - Explicit Close / Cancel (APP-UI-DIALOG-CANCEL-BUTTON); Esc on desktop where supported
  - Bundled offline HTML fallback (n78-legal/ or product-equivalent) when network is absent
- First-run gate: View Privacy / View Terms open the modal; acceptance checkboxes unlock
  after the operator opened each document in-app (not after external browser).
- Help menu Privacy and Terms use the same in-app viewer — not openExternalUri for legal URIs.

Allowed external browser (unchanged)
------------------------------------
- Support center / product hub / marketing URLs (APP-WEBSITE-LINKS-STANDARD)
- mailto: feedback and bug reports
- User-initiated store or check-for-updates links when policy says browser handoff is OK

Implementation pattern (KMP utilities)
------------------------------------
- readBundledLegalDocumentHtml(kind) expect/actual from desktop/android assets
- FamilyLegalDocumentDialog (or product-equivalent) at app shell root above worksheets
- openExternalUri must no-op or refuse nivo78-legal:// and must not browse policy/terms HTTPS
  from menu actions — route menus to the in-app dialog instead.

Verification
------------
- Manual: Legal gate -> View Privacy -> modal stays in app; Close returns to gate.
- Manual: Help -> Privacy -> same modal after acceptance.
- Guardrail: FamilyLegalGate.kt must not call openExternalUri for privacy/terms.
- Guardrail: desktop Platform must not call Desktop.browse for nivo78-legal://
