NIVO78 CANONICAL TRIAL MESSAGING — v1.0
=======================================
Rule IDs: FAMILY-TRIAL-TAGLINE-CANONICAL [STATIC], APP-TRIAL-TAGLINE-TRUTH [GATE],
APP-TRIAL-DISCLOSURE [GATE], APP-TRIAL-BILLING-DISCLOSURE [GATE]
Machine-readable: reference/NIVO78_TRIAL_MESSAGING.json
Behavior standard: reference/NIVO78_FULL_FUNCTION_TRIAL_STANDARD.txt (APP-TRIAL-FULL-FUNCTION)

Applies only to products that offer a time-limited trial.

APPROVED MESSAGING HIERARCHY
----------------------------
Primary Nivo78 trial tagline (family default):
  Try Everything. Decide for Yourself.

Supporting statement:
  Full access to paid features during your trial. No artificial usage limits. No obligation to buy.

Approved supplementary taglines (use when space and product fit allow):
  - Full Functionality. No Trial Restrictions.
  - Test It on Real Work. Then Decide.
  - Take Your Time. Make an Informed Decision.

TRUTHFUL USE RULES
------------------
- Primary tagline: use when the trial provides meaningful access to the paid functionality being evaluated.
- "Full Functionality. No Trial Restrictions.": use only when all advertised paid functionality is available
  and no material trial-specific restriction undermines that claim.
- "Test It on Real Work. Then Decide.": use when the product can be evaluated through realistic workflows.
- "Take Your Time. Make an Informed Decision.": must not imply a longer trial duration or unrestricted
  extension than the product actually provides.
- "No obligation to buy" (supporting statement): trial participation itself does not obligate purchase.
  Required payment details, automatic conversion, recurring billing, and cancellation requirements must be
  disclosed accurately before activation.

Do not imply every feature, integration, service, or capability is unrestricted when a legitimate
disclosed limitation exists.

Taglines are approved only when actual product behavior and disclosures support the claims
(FAMILY-MARKETING-CLAIM-EVIDENCE, APP-TRIAL-FULL-FUNCTION).
