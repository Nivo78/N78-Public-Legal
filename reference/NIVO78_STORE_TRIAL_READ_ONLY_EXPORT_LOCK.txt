NIVO78 STORE TRIAL — READ-ONLY / EXPORT-LOCK FREEMIUM
=====================================================
Rule IDs: APP-TRIAL-READONLY-EXPORT-LOCK, WEB-PRODUCT-TRIAL-AFTER-COPY
Primary projections: .cursor/rules/01-product-philosophy.mdc, 17-web-marketing.mdc
Hub layout: templates/PRODUCT_HUB_PAGE_LAYOUT.txt (clarity + Phase C after-trial paragraph)

Applies to: Nivo78 **store** utilities that ship a **time-limited evaluation** (trial).
Does not apply to: N78-Ops Play tier model (Basic / Pro / Premier) unless an explicit product override says otherwise.

Operator policy recorded: 2026-09-27 (Mike).

================================================================================
MODEL NAME
================================================================================

Read-Only / Export-Lock freemium (recommended default).

Not a hard time bomb that shuts down the entire app or hides the user's workspace.

================================================================================
TRIAL PHASE (30–60 DAYS — PRODUCT CHOOSES DURATION)
================================================================================

- Unrestricted access: create, edit, calculate, export — full product job.
- Trial length MUST appear in: product hub (how you pay), store listing, in-app trial status, and Help.
- In-app countdown or expiry date MUST be visible while the trial runs (no surprise midnight lock).

================================================================================
AFTER TRIAL — PERMANENT RESTRICTED MODE
================================================================================

Always allowed (free forever after trial):
- Open the app.
- View and read saved work: past calculations, machine setups, profiles, job logs, history.
- Navigate lists and detail screens for data the user already created.

Requires a license (paid unlock, subscription, or perpetual — per product):
- Create **new** records (or equivalent primary entities).
- Run **full** job calculations the product defines as licensed (e.g. full cycle-time run).
- **Export** and outbound handoffs: reports, G-code, CSV/PDF export pipelines, share sheets, "Save as…" from export flows.

Gate **write** and **export** paths; leave **read/list/detail** open.

================================================================================
DATA AND TRUST (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
================================================================================

- NEVER lock the user out of the app because the trial ended.
- NEVER silently delete, hide, or corrupt user data on trial expiry (see NIVO78_APP_STANDARD destructive-data: no auto-delete on trial expiry).
- NEVER use export lock without read access to existing work.
- Customer copy MUST state what stays free after trial and what requires payment — before install and in Help.

================================================================================
REINSTALL / TRIAL RESET (DESIGN INTENT)
================================================================================

- Do not rely solely on uninstall-blocking or opaque device fingerprinting.
- On typical mobile installs, uninstall removes app-private data (profiles, presets, logs).
- The model assumes **data gravity**: resetting the trial by reinstall costs more than paying — accumulated local utility outweighs clock-reset friction.
- Products MAY still persist entitlement via store receipt / Play Billing where applicable; UX remains read-only/export-lock after expiry regardless.

================================================================================
ANTI-PATTERNS (DEFECTS)
================================================================================

- "Trial expired — you cannot open the app."
- Paywall on opening old projects or viewing saved notes only because trial ended.
- Wiping workspace on expiry without explicit user-confirmed destructive action.
- Marketing that implies data will be deleted if the user does not pay (unless a separate, explicit, confirmed policy exists and is legally reviewed).
- Mid-task dark pattern: "Pay to save what you already typed" on data created during trial (export-lock applies to **new** export actions; existing read stays open).

================================================================================
PLAY / STORE COPY SNIPPETS (ADAPT PER PRODUCT)
================================================================================

How you pay (one sentence pattern):
  "{N}-day full access, then free forever to view your saved work; new jobs and exports need {SKU name}."

Will do — Free forever / after trial:
  "Open the app and read everything you already saved — setups, logs, and calculations stay on your device."

Will do — Trial:
  "{N} days of full create, edit, and export with no restrictions."

Will do — Paid:
  "Unlock new work and exports after the trial — {price model}."

Won't do:
  "Lock you out of your saved data when the trial ends."
  "Delete your work automatically when the trial ends."
